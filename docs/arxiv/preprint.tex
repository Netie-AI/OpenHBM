\begin{abstract}
We describe the architecture, verification methodology, and licensing
strategy of \emph{Netie Open HBM}: an open-source, JEDEC JESD270-4A-compliant
HBM4 memory subsystem (controller, PHY-shim, ECC, RAS, security wrapper)
tightly coupled to a RISC-V-native LPU accelerator. The project's thesis
is that trademark-enforced JEDEC compatibility, validated through a
demonstrably-running reference implementation under Apache-2.0, is
achievable on a 24--36-month horizon from outside the NVIDIA-/SK Hynix-
dominated stack, even though silicon supremacy is not. We describe the
controller's programmable XOR-hash address mapping policy (the
\texttt{addr\_map} block), the DRAM Refresh Management implementation
(\texttt{refresh\_mgr}), our SEC-DED + chipkill ECC stack
(\texttt{ecc}), the AI-agent training pipeline that generates the
majority of the verified RTL, and the open EDA + transistor-level
flows that close the toolchain gap when no PDK is available.
\end{abstract}
